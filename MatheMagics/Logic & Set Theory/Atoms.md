A single **string** in propositional form (P, Q, etc. ); **A propositional form** that consists only of a **propositional variable** ($\mathtt{P},\mathtt{Q},\mathtt{R},\dots$ and $\mathtt{P}_n,\mathtt{Q}_n,\mathtt{R}_n,\dots$)

Examples:  
P := The angle sum of a triangle equals two right angles  
Q := Some quadratic equations have real solutions
