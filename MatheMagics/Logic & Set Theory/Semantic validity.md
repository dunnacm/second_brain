## Definition:
### Verbal
Whenever the premise(s) are TRUE, the conclusion(s) must also be TRUE.
### Mathematical:
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_0, \ p_1, \ \ldots, \ p_{m-1} & \models q\\[5pt]
\Gamma & \vDash \varphi, \quad \mathtt{where} \ \ \forall \upnu \left[\, \upnu(\Gamma) = \top \;\Rightarrow\; \upnu(\psi) = \top \,\right]
\end{aligned}
$$

#### Inputs:
**Premises** and **conclusion(s)** are the two inputs to the **semantic validity** relation
#### Outputs:
Semantics (given a valuation v) outputs **T/F** for **propositional forms**
