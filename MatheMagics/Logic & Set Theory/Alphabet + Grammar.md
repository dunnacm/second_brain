## Alphabet + Grammar

### Big picture 
**Alphabet** + **grammar** as the core “syntax package” of a logical system:
- an **alphabet** gives the stock of symbols you are allowed to write, and
- a **grammar** (formation rules) selects which finite strings over that alphabet count as the *well-formed expressions* you will study.

In fact, he explicitly states the standard slogan:

> **A language = an alphabet combined with a grammar.**  
(Definition 2.1.13 in his Chapter 2 discussion of first-order languages.)

## Why “alphabet + grammar” is a useful note title
- **Alphabet** answers: *What symbols exist in my language?*
- **Grammar** answers: *Which strings count as terms/formulas?*
- **Semantics** (structures, assignments, truth) is defined **only after** the language (alphabet + grammar) has been fixed.
