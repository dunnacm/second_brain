## Rule 1 (Equality formulas)

### What the rule says
If $t_0$ and $t_1$ are **terms**, then
$$
t_0=t_1
$$
is an **atomic formula**.  
So equality is one way to turn terms into formulas.

### Why it matters
- Terms are “object-expressions.”
- Formulas are “truth-expressions.”
Equality is the bridge: it asserts that two terms designate the same object (under an interpretation and assignment).

### Examples
- $x=y$
- $f(x)=g(y)$
- $+(x,2)=y$ (often written $x+2=y$)
