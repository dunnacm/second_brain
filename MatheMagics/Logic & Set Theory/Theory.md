### Definition:

The term **theory** refers to a collection of well-formed sentences (propositions) in a formal language that all concern a particular *model*, *subject* or *field* in mathematics. The theory specifies:
- Which non-logical symbols (**[[Theory symbols]]**) are used
- Its [[Axioms]], the foundational propositions from which [[Theorems]] can be derived
Since different theories have different notation (think about how algebraic notation differs from geometric notation), **alphabets change depending on the subject matter**.
