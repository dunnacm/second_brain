## Definition:
> [!note] **Theorem — Existential Generalization (EG)**
>
> > [!warning] **IF**
> > - $p(x)$ is an $\mathcal{S}$-**formula**.
> > - $a \in \overline{\mathcal{S}}$.
>
> > [!tip] **THEN**
> > From $p(a)$ infer $\exists x\,p(x)$.

## Examples:
#### Legal uses:
- $p(a)\land \neg r(a) \;\Rightarrow\; \exists x\,[\, p(x)\land \neg r(x)\,]$
- $q(a)\land t(b) \;\Rightarrow\; \exists y\,[\, q(a)\land t(y)\,]$
