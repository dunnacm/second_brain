## Verbal definition:
A propositional form p is a tautology if v(p) always equals T for every valuation v
## Formal definition: 

> [!note]+ **Tautology**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > $p$ is a propositional form.
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > \vDash p \quad \Leftrightarrow \quad (\forall \upnu)\,[\upnu(p)=\mathtt{T}].
> > $$

## Example
$$
  \mathtt{P}\lor\neg\mathtt{P}
$$
