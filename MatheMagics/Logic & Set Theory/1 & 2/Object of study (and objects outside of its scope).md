## Definition & Object of study:

- The study of **Arguments**
	- Arguments = **(** Premises + Conclusions **)**
		- Conclusions follow from premises
- Legislates what counts as legitimate means from which one may to draw conclusion 
## Outside of its scope of study:

- **Non-propositional sentences** (Non-propositions)
