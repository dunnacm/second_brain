## Higher-arity predicates

## Definition
A **higher-arity predicate** is a predicate symbol of **arity $n$**, where $n\ge 2$.  
It applies to $n$ terms and expresses a **relation** among the corresponding subjects in the domain.

## Form
If $R$ is an $n$-ary predicate symbol and $t_0,\dots,t_{n-1}$ are terms, then
$$
R(t_0,\,t_1,\,\dots,\,t_{n-1}),\quad n\ge 2
$$
is an **atomic formula**.

## Examples
- **Binary predicate:** $<(x,y)$: “$x$ is less than $y$.”
- **Ternary predicate:** $\mathtt{Between}(x,y,z)$: “$y$ is between $x$ and $z$.”
