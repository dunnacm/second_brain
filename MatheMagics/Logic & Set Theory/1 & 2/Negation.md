Negates the truth value of a proposition ¬p
## Truth valuation
$$
\upnu(\neg \mathtt{p})=
\begin{cases}
\mathtt{T} & \text{if } \upnu(\mathtt{p})=\mathtt{F},\\[6pt]
\mathtt{F} & \text{if } \upnu(\mathtt{p})=\mathtt{T}.
\end{cases}
$$
