## Definition:
Propositional logic is the formal study of **valid reasoning with truth-valued statements** built from **propositional variables** (standing for whole assertions) and **logical connectives** (such as ¬, ∧, ∨, →). It captures the **truth-functional** part of mathematical reasoning—how conclusions follow from premises based only on logical form, without analyzing the internal structure of the statements.
