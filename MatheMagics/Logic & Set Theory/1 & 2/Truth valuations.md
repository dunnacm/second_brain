## Definition:
v: propositional forms → {T, F}
## Algorithm:

 -  Single **strings** in **propositional form**:
  $$
\vee(P) =
\begin{cases}
T & \text{if } P \text{ is true}, \\\\
F & \text{if } P \text{ is false}.
\end{cases}
$$

- **Compound propositions** in **propositional form**:
  $$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|}
\hline
p & q & p \land q \\\\
\hline
T & T & T \\\\
T & F & F \\\\
F & T & F \\\\
F & F & F \\\\
\hline
\end{array}
$$
