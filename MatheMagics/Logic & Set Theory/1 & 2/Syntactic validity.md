## Definition:
When the **conclusions** can be derived from the **premises** using the **proof apparatus**.  
$$
p_0, p_1, \ldots, p_{m-1} \vdash q
$$
Its **derivability** is based on formal rules and symbol manipulation.  

## Example:
**PREMISE 1**: If Hausdorff spaces are preregular, their points can be separated.
**PREMISE 2**: Hausdorff spaces are preregular
**CONCLUSION**: Their points can be separated
