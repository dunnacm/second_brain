It's concerned with form and structure. Reasoning purely from the arrangement of symbols, following **inference and replacement rules**, without appealing to the actual truth values. It establishes the rules of what constitutes valid forms of proof.
The validity of a deduction is judged by whether it follows the allowed **syntactic pattern**, not by evaluating truth tables.
