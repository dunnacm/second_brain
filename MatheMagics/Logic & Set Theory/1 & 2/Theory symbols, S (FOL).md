## Definition:

In first-order logic, ***theory symbols*** are the *non-logical* symbols—constant symbols, *n*-ary function symbols, and *n*-ary relation (predicate) symbols—that supply the subject-matter vocabulary of a theory. 
Unlike the ***logical symbols***, their meaning is not fixed universally; it is fixed only **relative to a structure** (a domain together with an interpretation), which assigns constants to domain elements, function symbols to actual functions on the domain, and relation symbols to actual relations on the domain.
