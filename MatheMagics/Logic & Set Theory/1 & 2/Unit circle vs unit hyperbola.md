To find the cotangent of the circular function, we draw a tangent at the intersection of the circle and the y-axis. However, the hyperbola does not intersect the y-axis. Therefore, we construct the **conjugate hyperbola** given by the equation y² - x² = 1. Draw a tangent at the point where the conjugate hyperbola intersects the y-axis (point B), extending it to meet the radius OP at Q'. As with the circular function, the length OQ' gives the ***hyperbolic cotangent***, or coth(A).

The ***hyperbolic secant*** (sech) and ***hyperbolic cosecant*** (csch) can be constructed using a conjugate hyperbola, though this construction differs from the circular case. Extend line XP to intersect the conjugate hyperbola at point P'. From P', draw a perpendicular to the y-axis, meeting it at Y'. Extend the tangent line AQ until it intersects line PY at point R. Draw line OR to intersect the conjugate hyperbola at point C. From C, draw a perpendicular to the y-axis, meeting it at M. The length CM represents the hyperbolic secant, or sech(A).
