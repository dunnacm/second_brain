## Definition
A **unary predicate** is a predicate symbol of **arity 1**.  
It applies to a single term and expresses a **property** of one subject (object) in the domain.

## Form
If $P$ is a unary predicate symbol and $t$ is a term, then
$$
P(t)
$$
is an **atomic formula**.

(So you can write $P(x)$ when the term is a variable $x$, but the term could also be a constant or a more complex term.)

## Examples
- $\mathtt{Even}(x)$: “$x$ is even.”
- $\mathtt{Prime}(x)$: “$x$ is prime.”
