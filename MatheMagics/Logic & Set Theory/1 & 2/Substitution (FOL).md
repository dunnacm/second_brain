## Fundamentals:
### Introduction:
There are times when a substitution will be made for a predicate's variable. For example, in algebra, if $f(x)=3x^2+x+1$, then $f(y)=3y^2+y+1$, $f(2)=3(2)^2+2+1$ and $f(sin(x))=3(sin(x))^2+sin(x)+1$. That is, one can substitute with variables, constants, and the results from functions. Therefore, to represent this in formulas, one can replace a variable with a term.
### Definition
It's the operation of replacing every **[[Free variables (FVs)]]** in a formula with a **S-terms**.
