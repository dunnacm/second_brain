- **Axioms** $\Rightarrow$ **Lemmas** $\Rightarrow$ **Theorems** $\Rightarrow$ **Corollaries**

Here “$\Rightarrow$” means “is **derived from**,” i.e., obtained by applying:
- **Inference rules** (derivation steps), and/or
- **Replacement** rules** (equivalence/substitution steps).
