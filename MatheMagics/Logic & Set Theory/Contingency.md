## Verbal definition:
A propositional form that is neither a tautology nor a contradiction is called a contingency
## Formal definition: 

> [!note]+ **Contingency**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > - $p$ is a propositional form.
> > - $p$ is neither a tautology nor a contradiction.
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > (\exists \upnu)\,[\upnu(p)=\mathtt{T}]
> > \;\land\;
> > (\exists \upnu)\,[\upnu(p)=\mathtt{F}].
> > $$

## Example
 $$
