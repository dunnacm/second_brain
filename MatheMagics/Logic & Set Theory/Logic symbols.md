## Definition:

Logical symbols (often: **logical constants**) are the reserved symbols of a logical language whose **logical role/meaning is fixed by the logical system itself**, independent of any particular theory, structure, or interpretation.

If _theory symbols_ are the _content_ (chosen per theory), **logical symbols** supply the _structure_ (fixed by logic).

> **Note (necessary clarification):** Variable symbols (e.g., propositional variables in PL) are part of the language’s alphabet, but they do **not** have a fixed truth/denotation; they receive values from a **valuation/assignment**, not from logic itself.
