## Theorem:

> [!note]+ **Logical equivalence**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > - $p,q$ are propositional forms.
> > - $\models\,(p\leftrightarrow q).$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$p\ \equiv\ q.$$

## Corollaries:
### Version 1

> [!note]+ **Mutual semantic consequence**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > - $p,q$ are propositional forms.
> > - $p\models q\ \text{and}\ q\models p.$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$p\ \equiv\ q.$$

### Version 2

> [!note]+ **Two-conditionals test**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > - $p,q$ are propositional forms.
> > - $\models\,(p\to q)\ \text{and}\ \models\,(q\to p).$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$p\ \equiv\ q.$$
