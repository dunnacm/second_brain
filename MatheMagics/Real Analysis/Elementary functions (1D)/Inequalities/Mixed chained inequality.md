| 1   | converse of a mixed chain                  | **» 1.1** *(flip ≤/<)*<br>$a\le x<b \;\Leftrightarrow\; b> x\ge a$.<br><br>**» 1.2** *(flip </≤)*<br>$a< x\le b \;\Leftrightarrow\; b\ge x> a$.<br><br>**» 1.3** *(mirror flip ≥/>)*<br>$a\ge x> b \;\Leftrightarrow\; b< x\le a$.<br><br>**» 1.4** *(mirror flip >/≥)*<br>$a> x\ge b \;\Leftrightarrow\; b\le x< a$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 2   | rigid shift (translate the band)           | **» 2.1** *(shift ≤/<)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\le x<b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c\le x+c<b+c$.<br><br>**» 2.2** *(shift </≤)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a< x\le b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c< x+c\le b+c$.<br><br>**» 2.3** *(mirror ≥/>)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x> b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c\ge x+c> b+c$.<br><br>**» 2.4** *(mirror >/≥)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a> x\ge b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c> x+c\ge b+c$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 3   | superset expansion (widen the band)        | **» 3.1** *(expand ≤/<)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c\ge 0,\\&a\le x<b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-c\le x<b+c$.<br><br>**» 3.2** *(expand </≤)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c\ge 0,\\&a< x\le b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-c< x\le b+c$.<br><br>**» 3.3** *(mirror expand ≥/>)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c\ge 0,\\&a\ge x> b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c\ge x> b-c$.<br><br>**» 3.4** *(mirror expand >/≥)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c\ge 0,\\&a> x\ge b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c> x\ge b-c$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 4   | scaling by a constant                      | **» 4.1** *(by $c>0$, ≤/<)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c>0,\\&a\le x<b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\le xc<bc$.<br><br>**» 4.2** *(by $c>0$, </≤)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c>0,\\&a< x\le b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac< xc\le bc$.<br><br>**» 4.3** *(by $c<0$, ≤/<)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c<0,\\&a\le x<b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\ge xc>bc$.<br><br>**» 4.4** *(by $c<0$, </≤)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c<0,\\&a< x\le b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac> xc\ge bc$.<br><br>**» 4.5** *(mirror, $c>0$, ≥/>)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c>0,\\&a\ge x> b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\ge xc>bc$.<br><br>**» 4.6** *(mirror, $c>0$, >/≥)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c>0,\\&a> x\ge b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac> xc\ge bc$.<br><br>**» 4.7** *(mirror, $c<0$, ≥/>)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c<0,\\&a\ge x> b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\le xc<bc$.<br><br>**» 4.8** *(mirror, $c<0$, >/≥)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c<0,\\&a> x\ge b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac< xc\le bc$.                                                                                                                                     |
| 5   | combine two mixed chains (sum/diff)        | **» 5.1** *(add: ≤/< with ≤/<)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\le x<b,\\&c\le y<d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c\le x+y<b+d$.<br><br>**» 5.2** *(add: </≤ with </≤)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a< x\le b,\\&c< y\le d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c< x+y\le b+d$.<br><br>**» 5.3** *(subtract: ≤/< with ≤/<)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\le x<b,\\&c\le y<d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-d\le x-y<b-c$.<br><br>**» 5.4** *(subtract: </≤ with </≤)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a< x\le b,\\&c< y\le d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-d< x-y\le b-c$.<br><br>**» 5.5** *(mirror add: ≥/> with ≥/>)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x> b,\\&c\ge y> d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c\ge x+y> b+d$.<br><br>**» 5.6** *(mirror add: >/≥ with >/≥)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a> x\ge b,\\&c> y\ge d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c> x+y\ge b+d$.<br><br>**» 5.7** *(mirror subtract: ≥/> with ≥/>)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x> b,\\&c\ge y> d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-d\ge x-y> b-c$.<br><br>**» 5.8** *(mirror subtract: >/≥ with >/≥)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a> x\ge b,\\&c> y\ge d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-d> x-y\ge b-c$. |
| 6   | product of two mixed chains ($>0$)         | **» 6.1** *(≤/< with ≤/<)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le a\le x<b,\\&0\le c\le y<d\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\le xy<bd$.<br><br>**» 6.2** *(</≤ with </≤)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0< a< x\le b,\\&0< c< y\le d\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac< xy\le bd$.<br><br>**» 6.3** *(mirror ≥/> with ≥/>)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x> b>0,\\&c\ge y> d>0\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\ge xy> bd$.<br><br>**» 6.4** *(mirror >/≥ with >/≥)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a> x\ge b>0,\\&c> y\ge d>0\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac> xy\ge bd$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 7   | ratios (division; positive denominators)   | **» 7.1** *(≤/< with ≤/<)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le a\le x<b,\\&0< c\le y<d\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{a}{d}\le\dfrac{x}{y}<\dfrac{b}{c}$.<br><br>**» 7.2** *(</≤ with </≤)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0< a< x\le b,\\&0< c< y\le d\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{a}{d}<\dfrac{x}{y}\le\dfrac{b}{c}$.<br><br>**» 7.3** *(mirror ≥/> with ≥/>)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x> b\ge 0,\\&0< c\ge y> d\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{a}{d}\ge\dfrac{x}{y}>\dfrac{b}{c}$.<br><br>**» 7.4** *(mirror >/≥ with >/≥)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a> x\ge b\ge 0,\\&0< c> y\ge d\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{a}{d}>\dfrac{x}{y}\ge\dfrac{b}{c}$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 8   | product of many mixed chains ($\ge 0$)     | **» 8.1** *(≤/< bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le a_i\le x_i<b_i\ \text{for }i=1,\dots,n\end{aligned}$ **then** $a_1\cdots a_n\le x_1\cdots x_n< b_1\cdots b_n$.<br><br>**» 8.2** *(</≤ bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0< a_i< x_i\le b_i\ \text{for }i=1,\dots,n\end{aligned}$ **then** $a_1\cdots a_n< x_1\cdots x_n\le b_1\cdots b_n$.<br><br>**» 8.3** *(mirror ≥/> bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a_i\ge x_i> b_i\ge 0\ \text{for }i=1,\dots,n\end{aligned}$ **then** $a_1\cdots a_n\ge x_1\cdots x_n> b_1\cdots b_n$.<br><br>**» 8.4** *(mirror >/≥ bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a_i> x_i\ge b_i\ge 0\ \text{for }i=1,\dots,n\end{aligned}$ **then** $a_1\cdots a_n> x_1\cdots x_n\ge b_1\cdots b_n$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 9   | powers of a mixed chain ($n\in\mathbb{N}$) | **» 9.1** *(≤/< in $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le a\le x<b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a^{\,n}\le x^{\,n}< b^{\,n}$.<br><br>**» 9.2** *(</≤ in $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0< a< x\le b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a^{\,n}< x^{\,n}\le b^{\,n}$.<br><br>**» 9.3** *(mirror ≥/> in $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x> b\ge 0\end{aligned}$ **then** $a^{\,n}\ge x^{\,n}> b^{\,n}$.<br><br>**» 9.4** *(mirror >/≥ in $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a> x\ge b\ge 0\end{aligned}$ **then** $a^{\,n}> x^{\,n}\ge b^{\,n}$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
