## Properties I

Let $m,n \in \mathbb{Z}$

## 1. integer “step” and sign equivalences

### 1.1

$\,n>0\;$ **iff** $\,n\ge 1$.

### 1.2

$\,n<0\;$ **iff** $\,n\le -1$.

### 1.3

$\,n>m\;$ **iff** $\,n\ge m+1$.

### 1.4

$\,n<m\;$ **iff** $\,n\le m-1$.

### 1.5

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $\,n<m\,$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$\,n=m-1\,$$
> > **or** $\,n<m-1$

## 2. growth bounds for integers

### 2.1

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n>1$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n^{2}\ge 2n$$

### 2.2

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n>1$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$2n>n+1$$

### 2.3

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n>1$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n^{2}>n+1$$

### 2.4

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n>1$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n(n+1)>n+2$$

### 2.5

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n\ge 3$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n^{3}>(n+1)^{2}$$

## 3. higher-degree / product dominance

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n\ge 3$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n^{3}>(n+1)^{2}$$

## 4. negative-range product

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n<-2$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n(n-1)>6$$

## 5. factorial/product chain

### 5.1

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n>3$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)>n^{2}$$

### 5.2

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n\ge 7$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n(n-5)>n$$

### 5.3

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n\ge 6$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$(n-1)(n-4)>n$$

### 5.4

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n\ge 5$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$(n-2)(n-3)>n$$

### 5.5

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n\ge 6$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n(n-1)\cdots(n-5)>n^{3}$$

### 5.6

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n\ge 7$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n!>n(n-1)\cdots(n-5)$$

## 6. factorial vs. powers

### 6.1

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n\ge 6$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n!>n^{3}$$

### 6.2

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n>3$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n!>n^{2}$$
