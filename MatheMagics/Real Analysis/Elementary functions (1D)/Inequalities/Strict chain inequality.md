| #   | Name/Theme                            | Property                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | converse of a chained inequality      | **» 1.1** *(flip chain)*<br>$a<x<b \;\Leftrightarrow\; b>x>a$.<br><br>**» 1.2** *(mirror flip)*<br>$a>x>b \;\Leftrightarrow\; b<x<a$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 2   | rigid shift (translate the band)      | **» 2.1** *(shift up)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a<x<b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c<x+c<b+c$.<br><br>**» 2.2** *(shift down, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a>x>b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c>x+c>b+c$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 3   | superset expansion (widen the band)   | **» 3.1** *(right/left expand)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c>0,\\&a<x<b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-c<x<b+c$.<br><br>**» 3.2** *(mirror expand)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c>0,\\&a>x>b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c>x>b-c$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 4   | scaling by a constant                 | **» 4.1** *(scale by $c>0$)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c>0,\\&a<x<b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac<xc<bc$.<br><br>**» 4.2** *(scale by $c>0$, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c>0,\\&a>x>b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac>xc>bc$.<br><br>**» 4.3** *(scale by $c<0$)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c<0,\\&a<x<b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac>xc>bc$.<br><br>**» 4.4** *(scale by $c<0$, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c<0,\\&a>x>b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac<xc<bc$.           |
| 5   | combine two chains (sum / difference) | **» 5.1** *(add bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a<x<b,\\&c<y<d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c<x+y<b+d$.<br><br>**» 5.2** *(add bands, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a>x>b,\\&c>y>d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c>x+y>b+d$.<br><br>**» 5.3** *(subtract bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a<x<b,\\&c<y<d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-d<x-y<b-c$.<br><br>**» 5.4** *(subtract bands, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a>x>b,\\&c>y>d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-d>x-y>b-c$. |
| 6   | product of two chains (positive)      | **» 6.1** *(both positive)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0<a<x<b,\\&0<c<y<d\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac<xy<bd$.<br><br>**» 6.2** *(both positive, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a>x>b>0,\\&c>y>d>0\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac>xy>bd$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 7   | ratios (division of two chains)       | **» 7.1** *(both positive)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0<a<x<b,\\&0<c<y<d\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{a}{d}<\dfrac{x}{y}<\dfrac{b}{c}$.<br><br>**» 7.2** *(both positive, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a>x>b>0,\\&c>y>d>0\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{a}{d}>\dfrac{x}{y}>\dfrac{b}{c}$.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 8   | product of many chains (all $>0$)     | **» 8.1** *(increasing bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0<a_1<x_1<b_1,\\&0<a_2<x_2<b_2,\\&\vdots\\&0<a_n<x_n<b_n\end{aligned}$ **then** $a_1\cdots a_n<x_1\cdots x_n<b_1\cdots b_n$.<br><br>**» 8.2** *(decreasing bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a_1>x_1>b_1>0,\\&a_2>x_2>b_2>0,\\&\vdots\\&a_n>x_n>b_n>0\end{aligned}$ **then** $a_1\cdots a_n>x_1\cdots x_n>b_1\cdots b_n$.                                                                                           |
| 9   | powers of a chain (natural exponents) | **» 9.1** *(inside positive band)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0<a<x<b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a^{\,n}<x^{\,n}<b^{\,n}$ for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$.<br><br>**» 9.2** *(decreasing positive band)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a>x>b>0\end{aligned}$ **then** $a^{\,n}>x^{\,n}>b^{\,n}$ for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$.                                                                                                                                                                   |
