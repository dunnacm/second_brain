## 13. [[Division of two inequalities (greater than)]]
### 13.1 Reciprocal nonzero
**If** $x>0$ **then** $\dfrac{1}{x}\ne 0$.
### 13.2 Zero below reciprocal
**If** $x>0$ **then** $0\ngtr\dfrac{1}{x}$.
### 13.3 Reciprocal preserves positivity
**If** $x>0$ **then** $\dfrac{1}{x}>0$.
### 13.4 Division by a positive preserves order
**If** $\begin{aligned}&w>0,\\&x>y\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{x}{w}>\dfrac{y}{w}$.
### 13.5 Reciprocal reverses order on (0, Infinity)
**If** $x>y>0$ **then** $\dfrac{1}{y}>\dfrac{1}{x}$.
### 13.6 Quotient monotonicity
**If** $\begin{aligned}&x>y>0,\\ &w>z>0\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{x}{z}>\dfrac{y}{w}$.
