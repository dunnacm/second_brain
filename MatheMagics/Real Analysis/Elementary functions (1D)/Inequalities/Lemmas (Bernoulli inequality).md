## 1. Bernoulli-type bounds for exponentials

### 1.1

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n\ge 0$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$2^{n}\ge n+1$$

### 1.2

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $n\ge 0$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$3^{n}\ge 2n+1$$

### 1.3

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $$
> > \begin{aligned}&n\ge 0,\\&a\ge 1\end{aligned}
> > $$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$a^{n}\ge (a-1)n+1$$
