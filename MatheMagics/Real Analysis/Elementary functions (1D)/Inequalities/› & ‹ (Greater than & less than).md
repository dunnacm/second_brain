## 1.. Multiplying an inequality by minus one

$x<y$  **Iff** $y>x$.

## 2.. Multiplication of inequality by a negative constant

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $$
> > \begin{aligned}&x<y<z\end{aligned}
> > $$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$x<z$$

## 3.. Converse of inequality

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $x<y$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$x+w<y+w$$

## 4.. Trichotomy

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $$
> > \begin{aligned}&x<y,\\&w<z\end{aligned}
> > $$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$x+w<y+z$$
