## 13.6 [[Quotient monotonicity]]
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Given: } \quad (1)\ x>y>0,\quad (2)\ w>z>0 \\[6pt]
&\text{Applying property 13.5 to eq.\ (2):} \quad (3)\ \dfrac{1}{z}>\dfrac{1}{w} \\[8pt]
&\text{Applying property 5 to eqs.\ (1),(3):} \quad (4)\ \dfrac{x}{z}>\dfrac{y}{w}
\end{aligned}
$$
