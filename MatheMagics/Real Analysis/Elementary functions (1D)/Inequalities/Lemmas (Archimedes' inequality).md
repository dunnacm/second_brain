## 1. two-sided quadratic squeeze

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $$
> > \begin{aligned}&n\in\mathbb{N},\\&n\ge 1\end{aligned}
> > $$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$(n-1)(2n-1) < 2n^{2} < (n+1)(2n+1)$$

## 2. upper bound (corollary)

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $$
> > \begin{aligned}&n\in\mathbb{N},\\&n\ge 1\end{aligned}
> > $$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$(n+1)(2n+1) > 2n^{2}$$

## 3. lower bounds (corollaries)

### 3.1

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $$
> > \begin{aligned}&n\in\mathbb{N},\\&n\ge 1\end{aligned}
> > $$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$n(2n-1) < 2n^{2}$$

### 3.2

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**

> > \begin{aligned}&n\in\mathbb{N},\\&n\ge 1\end{aligned}
> > $$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
