| #   | Name/Theme                                   | Property                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | converse of a chained inequality             | **» 1.1** *(flip chain)*<br>$a\le x\le b \;\Leftrightarrow\; b\ge x\ge a$.<br><br>**» 1.2** *(mirror flip)*<br>$a\ge x\ge b \;\Leftrightarrow\; b\le x\le a$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 2   | rigid shift (translate the band)             | **» 2.1** *(shift up)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\le x\le b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c\le x+c\le b+c$.<br><br>**» 2.2** *(shift down, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x\ge b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c\ge x+c\ge b+c$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 3   | superset expansion (widen the band)          | **» 3.1** *(right/left expand)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c\ge 0,\\&a\le x\le b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-c\le x\le b+c$.<br><br>**» 3.2** *(mirror expand)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c\ge 0,\\&a\ge x\ge b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c\ge x\ge b-c$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 4   | scaling by a constant                        | **» 4.1** *(scale by $c\ge 0$)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c\ge 0,\\&a\le x\le b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\le xc\le bc$.<br><br>**» 4.2** *(scale by $c\ge 0$, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c\ge 0,\\&a\ge x\ge b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\ge xc\ge bc$.<br><br>**» 4.3** *(scale by $c<0$)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c<0,\\&a\le x\le b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\ge xc\ge bc$.<br><br>**» 4.4** *(scale by $c<0$, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&c<0,\\&a\ge x\ge b\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\le xc\le bc$.                       |
| 5   | combine two chains (sum / difference)        | **» 5.1** *(add bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\le x\le b,\\&c\le y\le d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c\le x+y\le b+d$.<br><br>**» 5.2** *(add bands, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x\ge b,\\&c\ge y\ge d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a+c\ge x+y\ge b+d$.<br><br>**» 5.3** *(subtract bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\le x\le b,\\&c\le y\le d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-d\le x-y\le b-c$.<br><br>**» 5.4** *(subtract bands, mirror)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x\ge b,\\&c\ge y\ge d\end{aligned}$ **then** $a-d\ge x-y\ge b-c$. |
| 6   | product of two chains ($\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$) | **» 6.1** *(both nonnegative)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le a\le x\le b,\\&0\le c\le y\le d\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\le xy\le bd$.<br><br>**» 6.2** *(both nonpositive \& same signs)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x\ge b\ge 0,\\&c\ge y\ge d\ge 0\end{aligned}$ **then** $ac\ge xy\ge bd$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 7   | ratios (division of two chains)              | **» 7.1** *(positive denominators)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le a\le x\le b,\\&0< c\le y\le d\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{a}{d}\le\dfrac{x}{y}\le\dfrac{b}{c}$.<br><br>**» 7.2** *(mirror, positive denominators)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x\ge b\ge 0,\\&0< c\ge y\ge d\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{a}{d}\ge\dfrac{x}{y}\ge\dfrac{b}{c}$.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 8   | product of many chains (all $\ge 0$)         | **» 8.1** *(increasing bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le a_1\le x_1\le b_1,\\&0\le a_2\le x_2\le b_2,\\&\vdots\\&0\le a_n\le x_n\le b_n\end{aligned}$ **then** $a_1\cdots a_n\le x_1\cdots x_n\le b_1\cdots b_n$.<br><br>**» 8.2** *(decreasing bands)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a_1\ge x_1\ge b_1\ge 0,\\&a_2\ge x_2\ge b_2\ge 0,\\&\vdots\\&a_n\ge x_n\ge b_n\ge 0\end{aligned}$ **then** $a_1\cdots a_n\ge x_1\cdots x_n\ge b_1\cdots b_n$.                                                                                                 |
| 9   | powers of a chain (natural exponents)        | **» 9.1** *(inside nonnegative band)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le a\le x\le b\end{aligned}$ **then** $a^{\,n}\le x^{\,n}\le b^{\,n}$ for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$.<br><br>**» 9.2** *(decreasing nonnegative band)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&a\ge x\ge b\ge 0\end{aligned}$ **then** $a^{\,n}\ge x^{\,n}\ge b^{\,n}$ for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
