| 1   | order axioms & transitivity         | **» 1.1** *(reflexivity)*<br>$x\le x$.<br>**» 1.2** *(antisymmetry)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x\le y,\\&y\le x\end{aligned}$ **then** $x=y$.<br>**» 1.3** *(totality)*<br>$x\le y$ **or** $y\le x$.<br>**» 1.4** *(transitivity, non-strict)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x\le y\le z\end{aligned}$ **then** $x\le z$.<br>**» 1.5** *(transitivity, dissimilar I)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x<y,\\&y\le z\end{aligned}$ **then** $x<z$.<br>**» 1.6** *(transitivity, dissimilar II)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x\le y,\\&y<z\end{aligned}$ **then** $x<z$.                                                                                            |
| 2   | addition & subtraction              | **» 2.1** *(add constant)*<br>**If** $x\le y$ **then** $x+w\le y+w$.<br>**» 2.2** *(add two inequalities)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x\le y,\\&w\le z\end{aligned}$ **then** $x+w\le y+z$.<br>**» 2.3** *(subtract constant)*<br>**If** $x\le y$ **then** $x-w\le y-w$.<br>**» 2.4** *(subtract two: both non-strict)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x\le y,\\&w\le z\end{aligned}$ **then** $x-w\le y-z$.<br>**» 2.5** *(subtract two: dissimilar I)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x\le y,\\&w<z\end{aligned}$ **then** $x-z < y-w$.<br>**» 2.6** *(subtract two: dissimilar II)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x<y,\\&w\le z\end{aligned}$ **then** $x-z<y-w$. |
| 3   | multiplication by scalars           | **» 3.1** *(nonnegative scalar)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&w\ge 0,\\&x\le y\end{aligned}$ **then** $wx\le wy$.<br>**» 3.2** *(negative scalar)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&w<0,\\&x\le y\end{aligned}$ **then** $wx\ge wy$.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 4   | products & powers on $\mathbb{R}^+$ | **» 4.1** *(multiply two inequalities)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le x\le y,\\&0\le w\le z\end{aligned}$ **then** $xw\le yz$.<br>**» 4.2** *(multiply $n$ inequalities)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le x_1\le y_1,\\&\vdots\\&0\le x_n\le y_n\end{aligned}$ **then** $x_1\cdots x_n\le y_1\cdots y_n$.<br>**» 4.3** *(multiply three: one dissimilar)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0<x_1<y_1,\\&0\le x_2\le y_2,\\&0\le x_3\le y_3\end{aligned}$ **then** $x_1x_2x_3<y_1y_2y_3$.<br>**» 4.4** *(raise to $n$ on $\mathbb{R}^+$)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&0\le x\le y,\\&n\in\mathbb{N}\end{aligned}$ **then** $x^{\,n}\le y^{\,n}$.                |
| 5   | division & reciprocals              | **» 5.1** *(divide by positive constant)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&w>0,\\&x\le y\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{x}{w}\le\dfrac{y}{w}$.<br>**» 5.2** *(quotient monotonicity: both non-strict)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x\le y>0,\\&w\le z>0\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{x}{z}\le\dfrac{y}{w}$ (equality iff $x=y$ and $w=z$).<br>**» 5.3** *(quotient monotonicity: dissimilar I)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x\le y>0,\\&w<z>0\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{x}{z}<\dfrac{y}{w}$.<br>**» 5.4** *(quotient monotonicity: dissimilar II)*<br>**If** $\begin{aligned}&x<y>0,\\&w\le z>0\end{aligned}$ **then** $\dfrac{x}{z}<\dfrac{y}{w}$.        |
