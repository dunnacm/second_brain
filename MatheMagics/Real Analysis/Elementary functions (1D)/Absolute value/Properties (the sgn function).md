## 1. symmetry

$\operatorname{sgn}(-x) = -\,\operatorname{sgn}(x)$.

## 2. zero characterization

$\operatorname{sgn}(x)=0 \quad \Leftrightarrow \quad x=0$.

## 3. relation with absolute value

$x = \operatorname{sgn}(x)\,\lvert x\rvert$.

## 4. power identity

$$

\operatorname{sgn}(x)^n =

\begin{cases}

\operatorname{sgn}(x), & \text{if } n \text{ is odd},\\

0, & \text{if } n \text{ is even and } x=0,\\

1, & \text{if } n \text{ is even and } x\neq 0.

\end{cases}

$$

## 5. product decomposition

$\operatorname{sgn}(xy) = \operatorname{sgn}(x)\,\operatorname{sgn}(y)$.

## 6. quotient decomposition

> [!note]+ **Property**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $$
> > \begin{aligned}&y\ne 0\end{aligned}
> > $$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$\operatorname{sgn}\!\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right) = \operatorname{sgn}(x)\,\operatorname{sgn}(y)$$

## 7. relation with absolute difference

$\operatorname{sgn}(x-y) = \begin{cases} -1 & x<y, \\ 0 & x=y, \\ 1 & x>y. \end{cases}$
