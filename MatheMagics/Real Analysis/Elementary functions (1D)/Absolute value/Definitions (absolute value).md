## Piecewise definition
  $$
  |x| =
  \begin{cases}
    x, & x \ge 0,\\[2pt]
    -x, & x < 0.
  \end{cases}
  $$
## Square-root definition
 $$
  |x| = \sqrt{x^{2}}.
  $$
## Distance interpretation
- The distance between *x* and *y* on the real line:
	$|x-y| \text{ is the distance between } x \text{ and } y \text{ on the real line.}$

- In particularly, the distance from *x* to 0 is:
	$|x| = |x-0|$
## Definition via the sign function

$$
\lvert a\rvert = \operatorname{sgn}(a)\,a.
$$

Equivalently,
$$
a=\operatorname{sgn}(a)\,\lvert a\rvert,
\qquad
\operatorname{sgn}(a)=\frac{a}{\lvert a\rvert}\ \ (a\neq 0).
$$
