> [!note]+ **Half-Threshold Above for Positive Limit**
>
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > $\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty} \{s_n\} = L > 0$.
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > \exists N\in\mathbb{N}\ \forall n\in\mathbb{N}\,\big[\,n\ge N \Rightarrow s_n>\tfrac{L}{2}\,\big].
> > $$
