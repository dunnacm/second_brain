> [!note]+ **Order Preservation (Reverse Inequality)**
>
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty}\{s_n\}=K$
> > - $\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty}\{t_n\}=L$
> > - $\forall n\in\mathbb{N}\,[\,s_n\ge t_n\,]$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > K \;\ge\; L.
> > $$
