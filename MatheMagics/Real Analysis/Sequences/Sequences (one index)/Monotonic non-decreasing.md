> [!note]+ **Monotone (nondecreasing)**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > $\forall m,n\in\mathbb{N}\,[\,m\le n \Rightarrow s_m\le s_n\,]$
> 
> 
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > \forall n\in\mathbb{N}\,[\,s_n\le s_{n+1}\,].
> > $$
