> [!note]+ **Divergence via Two Subsequence Limits**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $\{s_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is a sequence,  
> > - there exist subsequences $\{t_n\}$ and $\{u_n\}$ of $\{s_n\}$ such that  
> >   $$\lim_{n\to\infty}\{t_n\}=L_1,\qquad \lim_{n\to\infty}\{u_n\}=L_2,\qquad L_1\ne L_2,$$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$\{s_n\}\ \text{does not converge (i.e., is divergent).}$$
