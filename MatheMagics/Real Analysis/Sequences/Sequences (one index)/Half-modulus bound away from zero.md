> [!note]+ **Half-Modulus Bound Away from Zero**
>
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > $\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty} \{s_n\}=L\ne 0$.
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > \exists N\in\mathbb{N}\ \forall n\in\mathbb{N}\,\big[\,n\ge N \Rightarrow |s_n|>\tfrac{|L|}{2}\,\big].
> > $$
