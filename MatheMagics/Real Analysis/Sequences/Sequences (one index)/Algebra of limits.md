Note that the sequences $\{s_n + t_n\}$, $\{s_n\cdot t_n\}$, and $\left\{\dfrac{s_n}{t_n}\right\}$ may converge even if $\{s_n\}$ and $\{t_n\}$ do not.

- Addition ⇔ Sum
- Subtraction ⇔ Difference
- Multiplication ⇔ Product
- Division ⇔ Quotient
- Radicals
