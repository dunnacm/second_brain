> [!note]+ **Limit respects an upper bound**
>
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty}\{s_n\}=K$
> > - Fix $c\in\mathbb{R}$ and $\forall n\in\mathbb{N}\,[\,s_n\le c\,]$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > K \;\le\; c.
> > $$
>---
> > [!note]+ **Notes**
> > - The converse is **false**: $s_n>c$ for all $n$ does **not** imply $K>c$.
