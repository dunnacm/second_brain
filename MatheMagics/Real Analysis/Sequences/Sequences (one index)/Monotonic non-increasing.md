> [!note]+ **Monotone (nonincreasing) — characterization**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > $\forall m,n\in\mathbb{N}\,[\,m\ge n \Rightarrow s_m\ge s_n\,]$
> 
> 
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > \forall n\in\mathbb{N}\,[\,s_n\ge s_{n+1}\,].
> > $$
