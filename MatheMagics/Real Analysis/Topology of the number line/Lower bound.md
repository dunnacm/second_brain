## Definitions:
### Verbal definition:
Think of **S** as dots on the number line. A number **ℓ** is a *lower bound* of **S** if it works as a floor: every element of **S** sits at or above **ℓ**. There can be many lower bounds; being a lower bound just means “nothing in **S** falls below **ℓ**.”
### Formal definition:

> [!note]+ **Lower bound — definition**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > - $S\subseteq\mathbb{R}\ \wedge\ S\neq\varnothing$
> > - $\ell\ \text{is a lower bound of }S$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > (\forall s\in S)\,[\,\ell\le s\,]
> > $$
