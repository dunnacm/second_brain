> [!note]+ **Definition 3.2.1 (Abbott) — Interior point**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $A\subseteq\mathbb{R}$,
> > - $x\in\mathbb{R}$.
> 
> 
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > x\in\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb{R}}(A)
> > \ \Leftrightarrow\
> > x\in A^\circ
> > \ \Leftrightarrow\
> > \exists \varepsilon>0\ [\,V_\varepsilon(x)\subseteq A\,].
> > $$
