> [!note]+ **Definition — Isolated point**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $A\subseteq\mathbb{R}$,
> > - $x\in A$.
> 
> 
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > \begin{aligned}
> > x \text{ is an isolated point of } A
> > &\ \Leftrightarrow\ x\in A\setminus A'
> > \\[6pt]
> > &\ \Leftrightarrow\ \exists \varepsilon>0\,[\,V_\varepsilon(x)\cap A = \{x\}\,]
> > \\[6pt]
> > &\ \Leftrightarrow\ \exists \varepsilon>0\,[\,V_\varepsilon(x)\cap (A\setminus\{x\}) = \varnothing\,].
> > \end{aligned}
> > $$
