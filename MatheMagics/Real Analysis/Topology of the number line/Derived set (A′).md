> [!note]+ **Definition — Derived set (set of limit points)**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > $A\subseteq\mathbb{R}$.
> 
> 
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > A'
> > \;=\;
> > \big\{\,x\in\mathbb{R} :
> > \forall \varepsilon>0\ \exists a\in A\,[\,a\neq x \land |a-x|<\varepsilon\,]
> > \big\},
> > $$
> > i.e., $A'$ is the set of all limit points of $A$ (relative to the ambient space $\mathbb{R}$).
