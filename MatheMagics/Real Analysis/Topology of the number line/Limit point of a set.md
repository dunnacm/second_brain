> [!note]+ **Definition — Limit point of a set**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $A\subseteq\mathbb{R}$,
> > - $x\in\mathbb{R}$.
> 
> 
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > \begin{aligned}
> > x \text{ is a limit point of } A
> > &\ \Leftrightarrow\ x\in A'
> > \\[6pt]
> > &\ \Leftrightarrow\ \forall \varepsilon>0\ \exists a\in A\,[\,a\neq x \land |a-x|<\varepsilon\,],
> > \end{aligned}
> > $$
>  
>  where $A'$ denotes the set of all limit points of $A$.
