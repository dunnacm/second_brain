## Definition:

> [!note]+ **Density of A in B**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - A, B are **ordered fields**.
> > - A is **dense** in B
> 
> 
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > \forall x,y\in B\,[\,x<y \Rightarrow \exists a\in A\,(x<a<y)\,].
> > $$
