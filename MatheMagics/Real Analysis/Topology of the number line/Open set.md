## Definitions:

> [!note]+ **Definition 3.2.1 (Abbott) — Open set**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $U\subseteq\mathbb{R}$.
> >
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > U \text{ is open}
> > \ \Leftrightarrow\
> > \forall x\in U\ \exists \varepsilon>0\ [\,V_\varepsilon(x)\subseteq U\,].
> > $$
>
>**Convention**:  
>Many authors prefer **$U$** for “open neighborhoods” / “open sets”. In these notes, I use:
>- $U$ for open sets,  
>-  $x$ for a generic point of $\mathbb{R}$ (and thus for points in $U$).
