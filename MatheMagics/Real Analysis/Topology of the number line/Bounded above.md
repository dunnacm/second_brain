## Definitions:
### Verbal definition:
A set **S** is *bounded above* if it has at least one ceiling: there exists some number **u** such that every element of **S** is at or below **u**. You don’t care which ceiling—only that at least one exists.
### Formal definition:

> [!note]+ **Bounded above — definition**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > - $S\subseteq\mathbb{R}\ \wedge\ S\neq\varnothing$
> > - $S\ \text{is bounded above}$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > (\exists u\in\mathbb{R})\,(\forall s\in S)\,[\,s\le u\,]
> > $$
