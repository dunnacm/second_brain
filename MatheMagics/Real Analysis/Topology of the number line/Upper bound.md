## Definition:
### Verbal definition:
Think of **S** as dots on the number line. A number **u** is an *upper bound* of **S** if it works as a ceiling: every element of **S** sits at or below **u**. There can be many upper bounds; being an upper bound just means “nothing in **S** goes past **u**.
### Formal definition: 

> [!note]+ **Upper bound — definition**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > - $S\subseteq\mathbb{R}\ \wedge\ S\neq\varnothing$
> > - $u\ \text{is an upper bound of }S$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > (\forall s\in S)\,[\,s\le u\,]
> > $$
