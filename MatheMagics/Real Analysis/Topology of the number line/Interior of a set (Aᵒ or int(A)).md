> [!note]+ **Definition 3.2.1 (Abbot) — Interior of a set**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > $A\subseteq\mathbb{R}$.
> 
> 
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > \operatorname{int}_{\mathbb{R}}(A)
> > \;=\; \operatorname{int} (A)
> > \;=\;
> > A^\circ
> > \;=\;
> > \{\,x\in\mathbb{R} : \exists \varepsilon>0\ [\,V_\varepsilon(x)\subseteq A\,]\,\}.
> > $$
