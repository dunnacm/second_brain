## Definitions:
### Verbal definition:
A set **S** is *bounded below* if it has at least one floor: there exists some number **ℓ** such that every element of **S** is at or above **ℓ**. You don’t care which floor—only that at least one exists.
### Formal definition:

> [!note]+ **Bounded below — definition**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > - $S\subseteq\mathbb{R}\ \wedge\ S\neq\varnothing$
> > - $S\ \text{is bounded below}$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > (\exists \ell\in\mathbb{R})\,(\forall s\in S)\,[\,\ell\le s\,]
> > $$
