> [!note]+ **Definition 3.2.2 (Abbott) — Closure of a set**
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > $A\subseteq\mathbb{R}$.
> 
> 
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > \operatorname{cl}_{\mathbb{R}}(A)
> > \;=\;
> > \operatorname{cl} (A)
> > \;=\;
> > \overline{A}
> > \;=\;
> > \{\,x\in\mathbb{R} : \forall \varepsilon>0\ [\,V_\varepsilon(x)\cap A\ne\varnothing\,]\,\}.
> > $$
