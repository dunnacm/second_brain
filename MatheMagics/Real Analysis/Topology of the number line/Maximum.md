## Definitions:
### Verbal definition:
Think of **S** as dots on the number line. A number **M** is the *maximum* of **S** if it is the topmost dot the set actually reaches: every element of **S** is at or below **M**, and **M** itself belongs to **S** (so **M** is the tightest ceiling that **S** attains).
### Formal definition:

> [!note]+ **Maximum — definition**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > - $S\subseteq\mathbb{R}\ \wedge\ S\neq\varnothing$
> > - $M=\max S$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > M\in S\ \wedge\ (\forall s\in S)\,[\,s\le M\,]
> > $$
