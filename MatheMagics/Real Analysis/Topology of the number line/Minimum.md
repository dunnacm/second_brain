## Definitions:
### Verbal definition:
Think of **S** as dots on the number line. A number **m** is the *minimum* of **S** if it is the bottommost dot the set actually reaches: every element of **S** is at or above **m**, and **m** itself belongs to **S** (so **m** is the tightest floor that **S** attains).
### Formal definition:

> [!note]+ **Minimum — definition**
> > [!warning]+ **IFF**
> > - $S\subseteq\mathbb{R}\ \wedge\ S\neq\varnothing$
> > - $m=\min S$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$
> > m\in S\ \wedge\ (\forall s\in S)\,[\,m\le s\,]
> > $$
