> [!note]+ **Limit comparison test — convergence (special case)**
>
> > [!warning]+ **IF**
> > - $S^{(1)}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$  
> >   ∘ $a_n\ge 0$ for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$ (i.e., **positive**)  
> >
> > - $S^{(2)}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n$  
> >   ∘ $b_n\ge 0$ for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$ (i.e., **positive**)  
> >   ∘ $S^{(2)}$ is **convergent**  
> >
> > - $\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{a_n}{b_n}=0.$
>
> > [!tip]+ **THEN**
> > $$S^{(1)}\ \text{is convergent.}$$
