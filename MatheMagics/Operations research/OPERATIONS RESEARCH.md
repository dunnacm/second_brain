## Basics
### Course 1 — **Supply Chain Logistics** (10 hours)

Covers the three major building blocks of logistics networks. You'll learn to differentiate the advantages and disadvantages of different transportation modes, understand warehousing facility design, and develop logistics networks that minimize costs while [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/supply-chain-management) delivering strong customer service.

**Key skills:** Logistics, Transportation, Warehousing & Inventory, Supply Chain Management

### Course 2 — **Supply Chain Operations** (12 hours)

Introduces and applies essential process improvement tools. You'll be able to apply the Six Sigma methodology, demonstrate the basic tenets of Lean philosophy to eliminate waste, and apply those tools to improve a real process. [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/supply-chain-management)

**Key skills:** Lean Six Sigma, Kanban, Process Improvement, Operations Management, Quality Improvement

### Course 3 — **Supply Chain Sourcing** (12 hours)

Covers the supplier selection process and supplier segmentation. You'll gain tools and techniques to find the right vendors and maintain productive relationships with existing suppliers. [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/supply-chain-management)

**Key skills:** Strategic Sourcing, Procurement, Supplier Relationship Management, Risk Management, Vendor Management
