## Business definition: 
Inventory that disappears without being sold (shrink, damage, expiration, write‑offs).
## Bathtub: 
A leak in the tub.
## Math:
An outflow $L(t)$ / $L_k$ in the on‑hand balance; a common stylized model is proportional leakage:
  $$L(t)=\delta\,I(t)\quad (\delta\ge 0).$$
