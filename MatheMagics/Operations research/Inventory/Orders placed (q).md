## Business definition

The replenishment action: how much you order now (or order‑rate).

## Bathtub

Turning the faucet handle / initiating refill.

## Math

### Control input

$q(t)$ (continuous) or $q_k$ (bucketed), typically chosen as a function of the planning state (often $IP$).
