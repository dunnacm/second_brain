## Business definition: 
Units arriving into on‑hand inventory.
## Bathtub:
The faucet inflow.
## Math: 
Receipts are the inflow $R(t)$ / $R_k$ that appears in the **OH** balance.  
When modeling lead time explicitly, receipts are a delayed function of orders $q$ (see **Lead Time** under Constraints/Delays).
