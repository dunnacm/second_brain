## Definitions:
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
\mathtt{sinh(x)} &:=& \mathtt{\dfrac{e^{x}-e^{-x}}{2}},\quad \mathtt{x\in\mathbb{R}}
\\[6pt]
\mathtt{cosh(x)} &:=& \mathtt{\dfrac{e^{x}+e^{-x}}{2}},\quad \mathtt{x\in\mathbb{R}}
\\[6pt]
\mathtt{tanh(x)} &:=& \mathtt{\dfrac{sinh(x)}{cosh(x)}},\quad \mathtt{x\in\mathbb{R}}
\end{array}
$$
