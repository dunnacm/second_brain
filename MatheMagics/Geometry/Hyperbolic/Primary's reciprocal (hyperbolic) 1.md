## Definition:
$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
\mathtt{sech(x)} &:=& \mathtt{\dfrac{1}{cosh(x)}},\quad \mathtt{x\in\mathbb{R}}
\\[6pt]
\mathtt{csch(x)} &:=& \mathtt{\dfrac{1}{sinh(x)}},\quad \mathtt{x\in\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}}
\\[6pt]
\mathtt{coth(x)} &:=& \mathtt{\dfrac{cosh(x)}{sinh(x)}},\quad \mathtt{x\in\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}}
\end{array}
$$
