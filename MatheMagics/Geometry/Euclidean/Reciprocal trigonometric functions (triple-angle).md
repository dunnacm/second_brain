## Triple Angle Identity (Cotangent):
### $\boldsymbol{\cot(3x)=\dfrac{3\cot x-\cot^3 x}{1-3\cot^2 x}.}$
**Assumption:** $\sin x\neq 0$ and $1-3\cot^2 x\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cot(3x)=\dfrac{1}{\tan(3x)} \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \tan(3x)=\dfrac{3\tan x-\tan^3 x}{1-3\tan^2 x} \\[10pt]
(3)\quad & \cot(3x)=\dfrac{1-3\tan^2 x}{3\tan x-\tan^3 x} \\[10pt]
(4)\quad & \cot(3x)=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{\tan^3 x}-\dfrac{3}{\tan x}}{\dfrac{3}{\tan^2 x}-1}
=\dfrac{\cot^3 x-3\cot x}{3\cot^2 x-1} \\[10pt]
(5)\quad & \cot(3x)=\dfrac{3\cot x-\cot^3 x}{1-3\cot^2 x}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cot(3x)=\dfrac{3\cot x-\cot^3 x}{1-3\cot^2 x}.}
$$
## Triple Angle Identity (Secant):
### $\boldsymbol{\sec(3x)=\dfrac{\sec^3 x}{4-3\sec^2 x}.}$
**Assumption:** $\cos x\neq 0$ and $4-3\sec^2 x\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cos(3x)=4\cos^3 x-3\cos x \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \sec(3x)=\dfrac{1}{\cos(3x)}
=\dfrac{1}{4\cos^3 x-3\cos x} \\[10pt]
(3)\quad & \sec(3x)=\dfrac{1}{\cos x(4\cos^2 x-3)}
=\dfrac{\sec x}{4\cos^2 x-3} \\[10pt]
(4)\quad & \cos^2 x=\dfrac{1}{\sec^2 x}
\quad \Rightarrow\quad
4\cos^2 x-3=\dfrac{4-3\sec^2 x}{\sec^2 x} \\[10pt]
(5)\quad & \sec(3x)=\dfrac{\sec x}{\dfrac{4-3\sec^2 x}{\sec^2 x}}
=\dfrac{\sec^3 x}{4-3\sec^2 x}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \sec(3x)=\dfrac{\sec^3 x}{4-3\sec^2 x}.}
$$
## Triple Angle Identity (Cosecant):
### $\boldsymbol{\csc(3x)=\dfrac{\csc^3 x}{3\csc^2 x-4}.}$
**Assumption:** $\sin x\neq 0$ and $3\csc^2 x-4\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \sin(3x)=3\sin x-4\sin^3 x \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \csc(3x)=\dfrac{1}{\sin(3x)}
=\dfrac{1}{3\sin x-4\sin^3 x} \\[10pt]
(3)\quad & \csc(3x)=\dfrac{1}{\sin x(3-4\sin^2 x)}
=\dfrac{\csc x}{3-4\sin^2 x} \\[10pt]
(4)\quad & \sin^2 x=\dfrac{1}{\csc^2 x}
\quad \Rightarrow\quad
3-4\sin^2 x=\dfrac{3\csc^2 x-4}{\csc^2 x} \\[10pt]
(5)\quad & \csc(3x)=\dfrac{\csc x}{\dfrac{3\csc^2 x-4}{\csc^2 x}}
=\dfrac{\csc^3 x}{3\csc^2 x-4}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \csc(3x)=\dfrac{\csc^3 x}{3\csc^2 x-4}.}
$$
