## Product-to-Sum Identity (Cosine · Cosine): 
### $\boldsymbol{\cos x\cos y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\cos(x-y)+\cos(x+y)\Big).}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cos(x-y)+\cos(x+y) \\[6pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =\big(\cos x\cos y+\sin x\sin y\big)+\big(\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y\big) \\[8pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =2\cos x\cos y \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \cos x\cos y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\cos(x-y)+\cos(x+y)\Big)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cos x\cos y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\cos(x-y)+\cos(x+y)\Big).}
$$
## Product-to-Sum Identity (Sine · Sine): 
### $\boldsymbol{\sin x\sin y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\cos(x-y)-\cos(x+y)\Big).}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cos(x-y)-\cos(x+y) \\[6pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =\big(\cos x\cos y+\sin x\sin y\big)-\big(\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y\big) \\[8pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =2\sin x\sin y \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \sin x\sin y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\cos(x-y)-\cos(x+y)\Big)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \sin x\sin y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\cos(x-y)-\cos(x+y)\Big).}
$$

#### Product-to-Sum Identity (Cosine · Sine): 
##### $\boldsymbol{\cos x\sin y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\sin(x+y)-\sin(x-y)\Big).}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \sin(x+y)-\sin(x-y) \\[6pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =\big(\sin x\cos y+\cos x\sin y\big)-\big(\sin x\cos y-\cos x\sin y\big) \\[8pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =2\cos x\sin y \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \cos x\sin y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\sin(x+y)-\sin(x-y)\Big)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cos x\sin y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\sin(x+y)-\sin(x-y)\Big).}
$$
## Product-to-Sum Identity (Tangent · Tangent):
### $\boldsymbol{\tan x\,\tan y=\dfrac{\cos(x-y)-\cos(x+y)}{\cos(x-y)+\cos(x+y)}.}$

**Assumption:** $\cos x\neq 0$, $\cos y\neq 0$, and $\cos(x-y)+\cos(x+y)\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \tan x\,\tan y
=\left(\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}\right)\left(\dfrac{\sin y}{\cos y}\right)
=\dfrac{\sin x\sin y}{\cos x\cos y} \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \sin x\sin y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\cos(x-y)-\cos(x+y)\Big) \\[10pt]
(3)\quad & \cos x\cos y=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\cos(x-y)+\cos(x+y)\Big) \\[10pt]
(4)\quad & \tan x\,\tan y
=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\cos(x-y)-\cos(x+y)\Big)}
{\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\cos(x-y)+\cos(x+y)\Big)}
=\dfrac{\cos(x-y)-\cos(x+y)}{\cos(x-y)+\cos(x+y)}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \tan x\,\tan y=\dfrac{\cos(x-y)-\cos(x+y)}{\cos(x-y)+\cos(x+y)}.}
$$
