## Definitions:
### Inverse sine (arcsine)

$$
\sin^{-1}x := y,
\qquad
x\in[-1,1],\ y\in\left[-\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{\pi}{2}\right],
\ \text{and}\ \sin y = x.
$$

### Inverse cosine (arccosine)

$$
\cos^{-1}x := y,
\qquad
x\in[-1,1],\ y\in[0,\pi],
\ \text{and}\ \cos y = x.
$$

### Inverse tangent (arctangent)

$$
\tan^{-1}x := y,
\qquad
x\in\mathbb{R},\ y\in\left(-\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{\pi}{2}\right),
\ \text{and}\ \tan y = x.
$$
