## Trigonometric Power Formula (Even Sine):
### $\boldsymbol{\sin^{{\color{red}{2n}}}(x)=\dfrac{1}{2^{{\color{red}{2n}}}}\binom{{\color{red}{2n}}}{n}+\dfrac{{\color{red}{2}}}{2^{{\color{red}{2n}}}}\sum_{k=1}^{n}(-1)^k\binom{{\color{red}{2n}}}{n-k}\cos\!\big(({\color{red}{2k}})x\big).}$

## Trigonometric Power Formula (Even Cosine):
### $\boldsymbol{\cos^{{\color{red}{2n}}}(x)=\dfrac{1}{2^{{\color{red}{2n}}}}\binom{{\color{red}{2n}}}{n}+\dfrac{{\color{red}{2}}}{2^{{\color{red}{2n}}}}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\binom{{\color{red}{2n}}}{n-k}\cos\!\big(({\color{red}{2k}})x\big).}$

## Trigonometric Power Formula (Odd Sine):
### $\boldsymbol{\sin^{{\color{red}{2n+1}}}(x)=\dfrac{2}{2^{{\color{red}{2n+1}}}}\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^k\binom{{\color{red}{2n+1}}}{n-k}\sin\!\big(({\color{red}{2k+1}})x\big).}$

## Trigonometric Power Formula (Odd Cosine):
### $\boldsymbol{\cos^{{\color{red}{2n+1}}}(x)=\dfrac{2}{2^{{\color{red}{2n+1}}}}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{{\color{red}{2n+1}}}{n-k}\cos\!\big(({\color{red}{2k+1}})x\big).}$
