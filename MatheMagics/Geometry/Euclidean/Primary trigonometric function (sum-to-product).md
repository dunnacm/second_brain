## Sum-to-Product Identity (Sine + Sine): 
### $\boldsymbol{\sin x+\sin y=2\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x-y}{2}\right).}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \sin x+\sin y
=\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}+\frac{x-y}{2}\right)+\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}-\frac{x-y}{2}\right) \\[10pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =\Big(\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)+\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)\Big) \\[8pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ \ \ \ +\Big(\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)-\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)\Big) \\[10pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =2\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \sin x+\sin y=2\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x-y}{2}\right).}
$$
#### Sum-to-Product Identity (Sine − Sine): 
##### $\boldsymbol{\sin x-\sin y=2\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x-y}{2}\right).}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \sin x-\sin y
=\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}+\frac{x-y}{2}\right)-\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}-\frac{x-y}{2}\right) \\[10pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =\Big(\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)+\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)\Big) \\[8pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ \ \ \ -\Big(\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)-\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)\Big) \\[10pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =2\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \sin x-\sin y=2\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x-y}{2}\right).}
$$
## Sum-to-Product Identity (Cosine + Cosine): 
### $\boldsymbol{\cos x+\cos y=2\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x-y}{2}\right).}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cos x+\cos y
=\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}+\frac{x-y}{2}\right)+\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}-\frac{x-y}{2}\right) \\[10pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =\Big(\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)-\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)\Big) \\[8pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ \ \ \ +\Big(\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)+\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)\Big) \\[10pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =2\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cos x+\cos y=2\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x-y}{2}\right).}
$$
#### Sum-to-Product Identity (Cosine − Cosine): 
##### $\boldsymbol{\cos x-\cos y=-2\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x-y}{2}\right).}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cos x-\cos y
=\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}+\frac{x-y}{2}\right)-\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}-\frac{x-y}{2}\right) \\[10pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =\Big(\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)-\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)\Big) \\[8pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ \ \ \ -\Big(\cos\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)+\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)\Big) \\[10pt]
& \qquad\ \qquad\ =-2\sin\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cos x-\cos y=-2\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x-y}{2}\right).}
$$
