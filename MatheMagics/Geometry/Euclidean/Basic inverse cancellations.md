## Basic inverse cancellations (always true on the standard domains)

### $\boldsymbol{\sin(\sin^{-1}x)=x.}$
**Assumption:** $-1\le x\le 1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cos(\cos^{-1}x)=x.}$
**Assumption:** $-1\le x\le 1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\tan(\tan^{-1}x)=x.}$
**Assumption:** $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cot(\cot^{-1}x)=x.}$
**Assumption:** $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

### $\boldsymbol{\sec(\sec^{-1}x)=x.}$
**Assumption:** $\lvert x\rvert\ge 1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\csc(\csc^{-1}x)=x.}$
**Assumption:** $\lvert x\rvert\ge 1$.
