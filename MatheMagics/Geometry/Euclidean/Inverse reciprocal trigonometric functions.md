## Definitions:
### Inverse cosecant (arccosecant)

$$
\csc^{-1}x := y,
\qquad
x\in(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty),\ 
y\in\left[-\frac{\pi}{2},0\right)\cup\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right],
\ \text{and}\ \csc y = x.
$$

### Inverse secant (arcsecant)

$$
\sec^{-1}x := y,
\qquad
x\in(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty),\ 
y\in\left[0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)\cup\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right],
\ \text{and}\ \sec y = x.
$$

### Inverse cotangent (arccotangent)

$$
\cot^{-1}x := y,
\qquad
x\in\mathbb{R},\ 
y\in(0,\pi),
\ \text{and}\ \cot y = x.
$$
