## Definitions:
![[Trigono_sine_en2.svg|300x300]]
### Sine:
$$
\sin(\theta)
  = \frac{\textcolor{green}{\text{opposite}}}
         {\textcolor{red}{\text{hypotenuse}}}
$$

### Cosine:
$$
\cos(\theta)
  = \frac{\textcolor{blue}{\text{adjacent}}}
         {\textcolor{red}{\text{hypotenuse}}}
$$


### Tangent:
$$
\tan(\theta)
  = \frac{\textcolor{green}{\text{opposite}}}
         {\textcolor{blue}{\text{adjacent}}}
$$
