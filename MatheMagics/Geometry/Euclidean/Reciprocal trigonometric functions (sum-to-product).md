## Identity:
### $\boldsymbol{\sec^2 x+\csc^2 x=\sec^2 x\,\csc^2 x.}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \mathsf{Pythagorian\ identity:}
\\[14pt]
& (1)\quad \mathtt{\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1}\qquad\qquad\mathsf{(Express\ in\ reciprocal\ terms)}
\\[14pt]
& \qquad\ \mathtt{\dfrac{1}{\csc^2\theta}+\dfrac{1}{\sec^2\theta}=1}
\\[18pt]
& \qquad\ \mathtt{\dfrac{\sec^2\theta+\csc^2\theta}{\csc^2\theta\cdot \sec^2\theta}=1}
\\[18pt]
& \qquad\ \Rightarrow (2)\quad \mathtt{\sec^2\theta+\csc^2\theta=\csc^2\theta\cdot \sec^2\theta}
\end{aligned}
$$
