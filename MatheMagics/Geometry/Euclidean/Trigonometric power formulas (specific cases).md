## Trigonometric Power Formulas (Power-Reduction / Power Identities)
### Power-Reduction (Squares)
$$
\boldsymbol{\sin^2 x=\dfrac{1-\cos(2x)}{2}.}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\cos^2 x=\dfrac{1+\cos(2x)}{2}.}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\tan^2 x=\dfrac{1-\cos(2x)}{1+\cos(2x)}}\quad (\cos x\neq 0,\ 1+\cos(2x)\neq 0).
$$
### Cubes
$$
\boldsymbol{\sin^3 x=\dfrac{3\sin x-\sin(3x)}{4}.}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\cos^3 x=\dfrac{3\cos x+\cos(3x)}{4}.}
$$
### Fourth Powers
$$
\boldsymbol{\sin^4 x=\dfrac{3-4\cos(2x)+\cos(4x)}{8}.}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\cos^4 x=\dfrac{3+4\cos(2x)+\cos(4x)}{8}.}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\sin^2 x\,\cos^2 x=\dfrac{1-\cos(4x)}{8}=\dfrac{1}{4}\sin^2(2x).}
$$
### Fifth Powers
$$
\boldsymbol{\sin^5 x=\dfrac{10\sin x-5\sin(3x)+\sin(5x)}{16}.}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\cos^5 x=\dfrac{10\cos x+5\cos(3x)+\cos(5x)}{16}.}
$$
