## Pythagorean Identity:
### $\boldsymbol{\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1.}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & (\cos x,\sin x)\ \text{lies on the unit circle} \\[6pt]
(2)\quad & \cos^2 x + \sin^2 x = 1
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1.}
$$
## Derived Pythagorean Identity: 
### $\boldsymbol{1 + \tan^2 x = \sec^2 x \quad (\cos x \neq 0).}$
**Assumption:** $\cos x \neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1 \\[6pt]
(2)\quad & \frac{\sin^2 x}{\cos^2 x} + \frac{\cos^2 x}{\cos^2 x} = \frac{1}{\cos^2 x} \qquad (\text{divide eq. (1) by }\cos^2 x)\\[6pt]
(3)\quad & \tan^2 x + 1 = \sec^2 x
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ 1 + \tan^2 x = \sec^2 x \quad (\cos x \neq 0).}
$$
## Derived Pythagorean Identity: 
### $\boldsymbol{1 + \cot^2 x = \csc^2 x \quad (\sin x \neq 0).}$
**Assumption:** $\sin x \neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1 \\[6pt]
(2)\quad & \frac{\sin^2 x}{\sin^2 x} + \frac{\cos^2 x}{\sin^2 x} = \frac{1}{\sin^2 x} \qquad (\text{divide eq. (1) by }\sin^2 x)\\[6pt]
(3)\quad & 1 + \cot^2 x = \csc^2 x
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ 1 + \cot^2 x = \csc^2 x \quad (\sin x \neq 0).}
$$
