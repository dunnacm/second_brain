## Definitions:
![[Trigonometric_functions_and_their_reciprocals_on_the_unit_circle.svg|375x235]]
### Cosecant (reciprocal of sine)

$$
\csc\theta := \frac{1}{\sin\theta},\qquad
\theta \in \mathbb{R}\setminus\{k\pi : k\in\mathbb{Z}\}.
$$

### Secant (reciprocal of cosine)

$$
\sec\theta := \frac{1}{\cos\theta},\qquad
\theta \in \mathbb{R}\setminus\left\{\frac{\pi}{2}+k\pi : k\in\mathbb{Z}\right\}.
$$

### Cotangent (reciprocal of tangent)

$$
\cot\theta := \frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta},\qquad
\theta \in \mathbb{R}\setminus\{k\pi : k\in\mathbb{Z}\}.
$$
