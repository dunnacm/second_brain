## Trig of inverse-trig (right-triangle style evaluations)
### $\boldsymbol{\sin(\cos^{-1}x)=\sqrt{1-x^2}.}$
**Assumption:** $-1\le x\le 1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cos(\sin^{-1}x)=\sqrt{1-x^2}.}$
**Assumption:** $-1\le x\le 1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\sin(\tan^{-1}x)=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}.}$
**Assumption:** $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

### $\boldsymbol{\tan(\sin^{-1}x)=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}.}$
**Assumption:** $-1<x<1$ and $x\ne 0$ (so the RHS is defined).

### $\boldsymbol{\cos(\tan^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}.}$
**Assumption:** $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

---

### $\boldsymbol{\tan(\cos^{-1}x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{1-x^2}}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $-1\le x\le 1$ and $x\ne 0$.

### $\boldsymbol{\sin(\cot^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}.}$
**Assumption:** $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cot(\sin^{-1}x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{1-x^2}}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $-1<x<1$ and $x\ne 0$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cos(\cot^{-1}x)=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}.}$
**Assumption:** $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cot(\cos^{-1}x)=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}.}$
**Assumption:** $-1<x<1$.

---

### $\boldsymbol{\tan(\cot^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $x\ne 0$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cot(\tan^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $x\ne 0$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cos(\sec^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $\lvert x\rvert\ge 1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\sec(\cos^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $-1\le x\le 1$ and $x\ne 0$.

### $\boldsymbol{\sin(\csc^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $\lvert x\rvert\ge 1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\csc(\sin^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $-1\le x\le 1$ and $x\ne 0$.

---

### $\boldsymbol{\sin(\sec^{-1}x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{x^2-1}}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $x>1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\sec(\sin^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}.}$
**Assumption:** $-1<x<1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cos(\csc^{-1}x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{x^2-1}}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $x>1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\csc(\cos^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}.}$
**Assumption:** $-1<x<1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\sec(\csc^{-1}x)=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2-1}}.}$
**Assumption:** $x>1$.

---

### $\boldsymbol{\csc(\sec^{-1}x)=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2-1}}.}$
**Assumption:** $x>1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\sec(\tan^{-1}x)=\sqrt{1+x^2}.}$
**Assumption:** $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

### $\boldsymbol{\tan(\sec^{-1}x)=\sqrt{x^2-1}.}$
**Assumption:** $x>1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\csc(\tan^{-1}x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{1+x^2}}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $x\ne 0$.

### $\boldsymbol{\tan(\csc^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x^2-1}}.}$
**Assumption:** $x>1$.

---

### $\boldsymbol{\sec(\cot^{-1}x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{1+x^2}}{x}.}$
**Assumption:** $x\ne 0$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cot(\sec^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x^2-1}}.}$
**Assumption:** $x>1$.

### $\boldsymbol{\csc(\cot^{-1}x)=\sqrt{1+x^2}.}$
**Assumption:** $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

### $\boldsymbol{\cot(\csc^{-1}x)=\sqrt{x^2-1}.}$
**Assumption:** $x>1$.
