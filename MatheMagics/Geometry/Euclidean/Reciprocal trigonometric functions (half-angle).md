## Half Angle Identity (Cotangent):
### $\boldsymbol{\cot^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{1-\cos x}.}$
**Assumption:** $1-\cos x\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cot^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
=\dfrac{\cos^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}{\sin^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)} \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \cot^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
=\dfrac{\dfrac{1+\cos x}{2}}{\dfrac{1-\cos x}{2}} \\[10pt]
(3)\quad & \cot^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{1-\cos x}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cot^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{1-\cos x}.}
$$


### $\boldsymbol{\cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{\sin x}{1-\cos x}.}$
**Assumption:** $1-\cos x\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
=\dfrac{\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}{\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)} \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \dfrac{\sin x}{1-\cos x}
=\dfrac{2\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}{1-\big(1-2\sin^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)\big)} \\[10pt]
(3)\quad & \dfrac{\sin x}{1-\cos x}
=\dfrac{2\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}{2\sin^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}
=\dfrac{\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}{\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}
=\cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{\sin x}{1-\cos x}.}
$$


### $\boldsymbol{\cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{\sin x}.}$
**Assumption:** $\sin x\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{\sin x}{1-\cos x} \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
=\dfrac{\sin x}{1-\cos x}\cdot\dfrac{1+\cos x}{1+\cos x} \\[10pt]
(3)\quad & \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
=\dfrac{\sin x(1+\cos x)}{1-\cos^2 x}
=\dfrac{\sin x(1+\cos x)}{\sin^2 x}
=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{\sin x}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{\sin x}.}
$$


### $\boldsymbol{\cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\csc x+\cot x.}$
**Assumption:** $\sin x\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \csc x+\cot x=\dfrac{1}{\sin x}+\dfrac{\cos x}{\sin x} \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \csc x+\cot x=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{\sin x} \\[10pt]
(3)\quad & \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{\sin x}
\quad \Rightarrow\quad
\cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\csc x+\cot x
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\csc x+\cot x.}
$$


### $\boldsymbol{\cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1+\sec x}{\tan x}.}$
**Assumption:** $\cos x\neq 0$ and $\tan x\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{\sin x} \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
=\dfrac{\dfrac{1+\cos x}{\cos x}}{\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}}
=\dfrac{1+\sec x}{\tan x}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1+\sec x}{\tan x}.}
$$


### $\boldsymbol{\cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{\tan x}{-1\pm\sqrt{1+\tan^2 x}}.}$
**Assumption:** $\tan x\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \tan\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{1+\tan^2 x}}{\tan x} \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
=\dfrac{1}{\tan\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}
=\dfrac{\tan x}{-1\pm\sqrt{1+\tan^2 x}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \cot\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{\tan x}{-1\pm\sqrt{1+\tan^2 x}}.}
$$



## Half Angle Identity (Secant):
### $\boldsymbol{\sec^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{2}{1+\cos x}.}$
**Assumption:** $1+\cos x\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \cos^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{2} \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \sec^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}
=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1+\cos x}{2}}
=\dfrac{2}{1+\cos x}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \sec^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{2}{1+\cos x}.}
$$



## Half Angle Identity (Cosecant):
### $\boldsymbol{\csc^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{2}{1-\cos x}.}$
**Assumption:** $1-\cos x\neq 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1)\quad & \sin^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1-\cos x}{2} \\[10pt]
(2)\quad & \csc^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)
=\dfrac{1}{\sin^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}
=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1-\cos x}{2}}
=\dfrac{2}{1-\cos x}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\therefore\ \csc^2\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=\dfrac{2}{1-\cos x}.}
$$
