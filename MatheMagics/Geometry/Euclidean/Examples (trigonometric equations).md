## 1
**Solve for** ***x***: $\left(\sqrt{3}+\tan(x)\right)^2=\left(2+\sec(x)\right)^2.$

**Solution**:
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \left(\sqrt{3}+\tan(x)\right)^2=\left(2+\sec(x)\right)^2. \\[16pt]
& 3+2\sqrt{3}\,\tan(x)+\tan^2(x)=4+4\sec(x)+\sec^2(x). \\[16pt]
& 3+2\sqrt{3}\,\tan(x)-4-4\sec(x)=\sec^2(x)-\tan^2(x). \\[14pt]
& 2\sqrt{3}\,\tan(x)-4\sec(x)=1+\sec^2(x)-\tan^2(x). \\[14pt]
& 2\sqrt{3}\,\tan(x)-4\sec(x)=2. \\[16pt]
& 2\sqrt{3}\,\tan(x)=2+4\sec(x). \\[16pt]
& 12\tan^2(x)=4+16\sec(x)+16\sec^2(x). \\[16pt]
& 12\big(\sec^2(x)-1\big)=4+16\sec(x)+16\sec^2(x). \\[16pt]
& 4\sec^2(x)+16\sec(x)+16=0. \\[14pt]
& \big(2\sec(x)+4\big)^2=0. \\[14pt]
& \big(\sec(x)+2\big)^2=0. \\[16pt]
& \sec(x)=-2. \\[14pt]
& \cos(x)=-\dfrac{1}{2}. \\[14pt]
& x=\dfrac{2\pi}{3},\ \dfrac{4\pi}{3}.
\end{aligned}
$$
