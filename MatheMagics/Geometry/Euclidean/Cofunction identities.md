## Unit circle perspective:
### Graphical interpretation
![[Pasted image 20251130221921.png|300]]
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin B &= \frac{b}{c} = \cos A, &
\cos B &= \frac{a}{c} = \sin A, &
\tan B &= \frac{b}{a} = \cot A, \\[6pt]
\csc B &= \frac{c}{b} = \sec A, &
\sec B &= \frac{c}{a} = \csc A, &
\cot B &= \frac{a}{b} = \tan A.
\end{aligned}
$$

### Analytical approach:
#### **Complementary angle identities** $(\frac{\pi}{2} - x)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin x &= \cos\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right), &
\cos x &= \sin\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right), &
\tan x &= \cot\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right), \\[6pt]
\csc x &= \sec\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right), &
\sec x &= \csc\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right), &
\cot x &= \tan\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-x\right).
\end{aligned}
$$
#### Identities for $(\frac{\pi}{2} + x)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+x\right) &= \cos x, &
\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+x\right) &= -\sin x, &
\tan\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+x\right) &= -\cot x, \\[6pt]
\csc\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+x\right) &= \sec x, &
\sec\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+x\right) &= -\csc x, &
\cot\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+x\right) &= -\tan x.
\end{aligned}
$$
#### Identities for $(\pi - x)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin(\pi - x) &= \sin x, &
\cos(\pi - x) &= -\cos x, &
\tan(\pi - x) &= -\tan x, \\[6pt]
\csc(\pi - x) &= \csc x, &
\sec(\pi - x) &= -\sec x, &
\cot(\pi - x) &= -\cot x.
\end{aligned}
$$
#### Identities for $(\pi + x)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin(\pi + x) &= -\sin x, &
\cos(\pi + x) &= -\cos x, &
\tan(\pi + x) &= \tan x, \\[6pt]
\csc(\pi + x) &= -\csc x, &
\sec(\pi + x) &= -\sec x, &
\cot(\pi + x) &= \cot x.
\end{aligned}
$$
#### Identities for $(\frac{3\pi}{2} - x)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}-x\right) &= -\cos x, &
\cos\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}-x\right) &= -\sin x, &
\tan\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}-x\right) &= \cot x, \\[6pt]
\csc\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}-x\right) &= -\sec x, &
\sec\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}-x\right) &= -\csc x, &
\cot\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}-x\right) &= \tan x.
\end{aligned}
$$
#### Identities for $(\frac{3\pi}{2} + x)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}+x\right) &= -\cos x, &
\cos\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}+x\right) &= \sin x, &
\tan\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}+x\right) &= -\cot x, \\[6pt]
\csc\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}+x\right) &= -\sec x, &
\sec\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}+x\right) &= \csc x, &
\cot\!\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}+x\right) &= -\tan x.
\end{aligned}
$$
#### Identities for $(2\pi - x)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin(2\pi - x) &= -\sin x, &
\cos(2\pi - x) &= \cos x, &
\tan(2\pi - x) &= -\tan x, \\[6pt]
\csc(2\pi - x) &= -\csc x, &
\sec(2\pi - x) &= \sec x, &
\cot(2\pi - x) &= -\cot x.
\end{aligned}
$$

\cos x \Leftrightarrow \sin(x) \, = \cos\left( x - \frac{\pi}{2} \right)
