## Symmetry & parity (even/odd + periodicity)
### Even/odd properties:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin(-\theta) &= -\sin\theta,  &\qquad \cos(-\theta) &= \cos\theta,  &\qquad \tan(-\theta) &= -\tan\theta,\\[4pt]
\csc(-\theta) &= -\csc\theta, &\qquad \sec(-\theta) &= \sec\theta, &\qquad \cot(-\theta) &= -\cot\theta.
\end{aligned}
$$
### Periodicity:
Using the unit circle (one full revolution is $2\pi$ radians):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin(x+2\pi) &= \sin x,\\[4pt]
\cos(x+2\pi) &= \cos x,\\[4pt]
\tan(x+\pi) &= \tan x \quad (\text{since } \tan x = \tfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}).
\end{aligned}
$$

So their fundamental periods are:

- $\sin x$: period $2\pi$.
- $\cos x$: period $2\pi$.
- $\tan x$: period $\pi$.

### Summary table (parity and period)

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{Function} & \text{Parity} & \text{Graph symmetry} & \text{Fundamental period} \\ \hline
\sin x & \text{odd} & \text{origin symmetry} & 2\pi \\[2pt]
\cos x & \text{even} & \text{$y$–axis symmetry} & 2\pi \\[2pt]
\tan x & \text{odd} & \text{origin symmetry} & \pi
\end{array}
$$
