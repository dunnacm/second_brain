## D) Image → LaTeX (proof) transcription protocol (your main workflow)

### D1) Output formatting

- Entire proof in:
    
    - `$$\begin{aligned} ... \end{aligned}$$`
        
- **Every row begins with `&`**, including narrative rows.
    
- Equation labels are formatted as:
    
    - `(n)\quad & ...` (label before the alignment tab)
        
- Narrative/section labels (e.g., “Sub. (3),(4) into (1):”) appear as their own rows, formatted like:
    
    - `& \mathtt{...}`
        

### D2) Alignment + indentation rules

- Use **manual spacing** (`\quad`, `\qquad`, `\,`) to visually align:
    
    - continuation equals signs,
        
    - right-side parenthetical comments,
        
    - implication/biconditional markers.
        
- Derived implication lines must be **visibly nested**, e.g.:
    
    - `& \Rightarrow (7)\quad ...`
        
    - `& \qquad\ \Rightarrow (8)\quad ...` (one level deeper than its parent)
        
- Standalone case headings must be their own lines **without** leading `&` only if you explicitly want them that way; otherwise keep the project-wide “every row begins with &” rule.
    
- Use spacing markers judiciously:
    
    - default `\\[12pt]`, `\\[18pt]`, `\\[22pt]` (avoid overly large gaps unless the source demands it).
        

### D3) “Kaizen” rule (gold-standard matching)

When you provide a “closer to what I needed” block, that becomes the **formatting gold standard**:

- Preserve step labels (including repeats and primes), narrative phrasing, punctuation, and spacer pattern.
    
- Do not restructure the proof.
    
- Only change what is necessary for correctness or what you explicitly request.
    

### D4) Mandatory math-coherence scan (before finalizing)

I will actively check and correct:

- **Radicands/signs stay consistent** (e.g., `x^2-y^2=1` implies `\sqrt{x^2-1}`, not `\sqrt{x^2+1}` later).
    
- Differentiation steps match the prior expression.
    
- Substitution choice matches the integrand (e.g., `x=\sec u` fits `\sqrt{x^2-1}`; `x=\tan u` or `x=\sinh u` fits `\sqrt{x^2+1}`).
    
- If the source image has a math mistake, I will **flag + correct** it (not silently propagate it).
